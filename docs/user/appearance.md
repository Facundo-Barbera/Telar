# Appearance

Telar's look is one composition you can wear whole or change piece by piece. Everything is in Settings → Appearance, and every change applies right away. There's no Apply button.

## Looks

A Look is a complete appearance saved together: the colours for light and dark, the layers behind them, the accent, the two typefaces and their sizes, the depth, and how much of the layers shows through.

- Telar ships ten built-in Looks (Telar, Ember, Grove, Tide, Iris, Dusk, Deep Sea, Meadow, Orchid, Emberglow). You can't delete them.
- Click a Look's name to wear it. You can still change anything afterwards.
- Save keeps what the window is wearing now as a new Look. You can keep up to 12 of your own.
- Export a Look to a file, and import one to share it between machines or people.

You can also wear a Look from the command palette (⌘K → Wear look…).

## The composer

This is what a Look is made of.

- **Base**: the app colour. It sets the hue and how colourful the surfaces are, and Telar keeps text readable on top of it.
- **Layers**: linear gradients, radial gradients and images stacked over the base. You can reorder them, fade them, and repeat one across the window.
- **Match the other state**: copies the current state's layers to the other one (light or dark). Each state keeps its own base colour.
- **Adjust colours**: the individual colours, folded away. Use them to override one colour the base got wrong.

The composer edits whichever state the window is showing. To edit dark, switch the window to dark first.

## Type and surfaces

- **Accent**: the colour for things you act on, like buttons, links and the caret. There are eight to choose from.
- **Interface font** and **Code font**, each with its own size. Text size runs from 13 to 18 px, and you can step it from the command palette.
- **Depth**: how far cards, the composer and menus lift off the page.

## Window

These settings belong to this window. They don't travel in a Look.

- **Colour scheme**: Light, Dark, or System (follows macOS).
- **Translucency**: lets the desktop show through the window. The desktop app on macOS only. Turning it on rebuilds the window.
- **Glass**: Blur or Clear, when translucency is on.
- **Layers through canvas and rail**: how much of the layers (and the desktop, when translucent) shows through. This one is saved in a Look.

## Where it's kept

Appearance is saved per browser. The desktop app and each browser you open Telar in keep their own. If you open Telar from another device, Appearance offers **Follow the host's look**. Turn it on to wear the Mac's Look and keep up as it changes. Changing anything locally stops following.

The iPhone app doesn't take on your Look.
